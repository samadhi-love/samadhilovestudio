SAMADHI LOVE STUDIO · FIRST-TIME SETUP

No commands.
Just copy and paste.

This creates a new Studio vault key in your browser. It is free, and it is not your ElevenLabs API key.

Only for your first vault key.

If STUDIO_ELEVENLABS_VAULT_KEY already exists in Secrets or your Cloudflare settings, stop and keep the existing key. Replacing it can make saved account connections unreadable.

1. Copy the setting name

In the Replit editor, open Tools → Secrets → New Secret.

Click below, then click the Key field in Secrets and press Ctrl+V.

2. Create and copy your secret

After pasting the setting name, come back here and click below.

Then click the Value field in Secrets, press Ctrl+V, and click Add Secret. Do not paste it into chat.

Your key is never displayed or sent to a server by this page. It stays only in this tab’s memory and your clipboard. Keep this tab open until you have saved it. You can copy the same key again below and keep a backup in your password manager.

If copying is blocked in the preview, open this helper in a separate tab before generating your key.
