# Versual Studio — current guide

## Your songs: one action menu

Use **⋯** on a song for Play, Edit in Shape, Make Video, Save Voice, Share, Publish, Download, Duplicate, Add to Project/Playlist and Delete. Supported Keep Going and Redo tools are grouped with editing. More tools keeps the remaining specialist functions. Old views/direct actions still work; there is no second song library.

**Shape** is the existing waveform editor with Trim, Fade Out, Cut, Copy, Paste and Move. Preview first, then save a new version; the original is kept. Undo discards an unsaved preview, restores a selection or returns to the earlier recording without deleting the newer one. The private in-memory region clipboard lasts for the tab session. Copy/move captions use the actual crossfade assembly; changed portions need review. Pasting a different recording can require manually reviewing/replacing its words.

**Duplicate** copies the owned audio into its own private R2 object, along with writing and matching captions. It is not a paid generation and does not copy public releases, cover files, exported videos or original hum takes. A save failure keeps its audio and recovery record. Both originals remain available.

**Save Voice** means a private vocal idea, not identity cloning. Listen to the song, choose a position (or start/end), edit its voice description and confirm permission. A short independent clip and description save in Your Sounds; reusing the sound applies the description only. Reference clips never go to the music engine. Up to 64 private references, 30 seconds/2 MB each; nothing is deleted when the collection is full. Saved reference audio loads only after Listen is pressed.

**Record → Layer over song** sends the selected private take to the existing Mix & Edit, alongside the chosen song as a second real track. Cancel or a failed combined memory/track check leaves an open mix untouched. All recorder modes and multiple takes remain available.

**Download** contains original/converted MP3/WAV and other supported formats, saved videos, stems/MIDI and the release package. Private Share does not publish anything; Publish still opens the existing explicit release checks. Delete is reversible for 30 days, including older shorter windows; permanent cleanup remains disabled.

**Unsupported AI controls are hidden:** native ACE-Step 1.5 repaint, cover, remaster, melody following and voice cloning are not documented for the selected endpoint. Local editing/manual replacement still works. ElevenLabs offers the existing fresh replacement/continuation passage, not audio-conditioned inpainting or a guaranteed identical singer. Edit Words with native re-singing is not falsely advertised.

## Begin a song

**Just Begin:** write Your Vision, choose a singing language if needed, optionally turn on No Vocals, then press Begin. English remains the automatic default. Two takes are made by default; switch to Shape It to choose one. ElevenLabs asks before making two paid versions.

**Shape It:** edit Your Words or press Write them for me to review generated lyrics first; choose Sound & Soul and Voice directions; optionally name the song. Search, See more and free text expose the full sound collection. Complete selections are kept; the first roughly twenty guide the music. More contains supported Leave Out/Spark controls, Auto length up to six minutes and dialect options. Unsupported controls are hidden rather than simulated.

**Your Sounds:** the former saved prompts and Style DNA library now share one place. Save a named combination, optionally include words/vision, and apply it later. Leave Out, Spark and voice directions return with it. Existing entries remain usable. Studio tools contains the existing recipes, private melody notes, helpers, song map, musical ads and detailed production.

Both takes save privately and can be compared side by side; choosing a favourite does not delete the other. New song starts a fresh form; unfinished writing is quietly kept without recovery prompts.

## Owner-only ACE-Step music

In Advanced view, open Settings and choose **ACE-Step 1.5 · thinking quality**. Add the fal.ai API key as **FAL_KEY** in Replit Secrets and refresh engine status; it never belongs in chat or a client field. This checks configuration only, not paid generation. Without that key, the owner keeps ElevenLabs with a quiet explanation. Other creators continue using their own ElevenLabs keys.

ACE-Step defaults to two takes in one request; turn off Two takes in Shape It for one. Both takes are private in Your songs and can be compared side by side; keeping a favorite never deletes the other. Auto uses Studio's duration planner (the fal endpoint does not document native Auto), with a six-minute maximum. Longer directions/lyrics are not silently cut: the app uses ElevenLabs for inputs that fal cannot accept.

For a description-only vocal song, the existing ElevenLabs connection writes lyrics first. If it is disconnected, supply lyrics for ACE-Step. Automatic caption alignment stays on ElevenLabs; otherwise use the existing rough/manual timing. Private hum references are not sent to this text-only music endpoint.

If a request may already have reached fal.ai, Studio stops rather than automatically spending again. Check fal.ai history and Your songs before confirming Try again. Accepted queue references remain private in the saved job. A definite rejected fal submission can use ElevenLabs; that backup saves one take and does not automatically spend on a second.

The current checks are offline fixtures, not proof of live musical quality, dialect fidelity, provider billing, or playback on a real phone.

## Singing language starts with English

Create shows a small **Language** selector in both Simple and Custom, initially **Auto · English by default**. Priority is: **your dropdown choice → a language or dialect named in your description → clearly non-English lyrics → English**. The description matcher uses the same complete language/dialect names as the selector, including world-music languages such as Yoruba and aliases such as Brazilian Portuguese. Mood, genre and instrumental directions still shape the music. A quiet line always explains the result, for example **Singing in Spanish (from your description) · change**. Selecting English or any other listed language pins your choice; return to Auto to follow the description again. Short or uncertain text and conflicting descriptions never require resolving a language error. Your words are not translated. Dialect controls remain inside Custom → Vocals; provider pronunciation and dialect quality remain unverified, not guaranteed.

## Song length and credits

**Length: Auto** is the starting choice in Create. Your lyrics and song sections set a natural length. If you only describe a song, the composition planner uses about three minutes because it requires a numeric target. A specific time in your description is respected; turn Auto off to pick your own length. New songs, presets and **Make it longer** stay between **0:03 and 6:00**; crossfades count toward an extension's final length. Existing longer recordings remain saved.

Your personal ElevenLabs key pays for generation. A quiet line near Create says **Uses your ElevenLabs credits**; **Make 2 versions** still asks you to confirm double usage. Failed/uncertain paid attempts keep their separate recovery protections and are never automatically replayed.

## Writing returns automatically

Create quietly keeps your description, lyrics and choices. Returning to the screen opens the last writing without a restore/dismiss question. **New song** clears the form immediately, including the folded detailed-production form; returning again keeps it clear. Previous private device copies and saved songs are retained, not deleted.

There is no drafts list or recovery panel to manage. Internal account-writing records are kept behind the scenes, not shown as songs or picker options. Device writing takes priority over an older account copy, and late account responses do not replace newly typed words. A real conflicting account save still protects the other tab's changes and keeps this tab's words locally.

Lyric/video editors also resume applicable device edits automatically. Video recovery only applies to the same recording and unchanged saved version; a previous private copy is preserved. Previously paid caption-sync results resume without another provider call. Paid-song recovery continues automatically when Your songs opens, without a recovery prompt or manual drafts list. A completed recovery scan stays limited to once per session, but an incomplete pass or a later interrupted creation allows another silent scan on the next visit. No music generation is replayed automatically.

## Start here

Studio is for beginners first. Follow one path: **Create → My Songs → Video**. Record your own performance when you want to; Explore is for listening and discovering other work. Existing production engines remain available, but related tools now belong together.

The main navigation is **Create, My Songs, Video, Record, Explore**, with **Settings & Help**. The existing **Pro tools** switch reveals **World Sounds, Mix & Edit, Promote** and deeper controls inside the same screens. It does not create a second copy of the Studio or change your saved projects.

### Create

Choose an optional **Start from a recipe** template, describe the song, choose style chips and press Create. Use **Simple** for the shortest path. **Custom** opens separate lyrics, style, vocals and prompt-helper fold-outs when you want control. Language and singing-arrangement overrides are inside **Custom → Vocals**. The original detailed engine is inside **Custom → Pro production**, not another Create page; it stays mounted when folded away. Saved templates, prompts and lyric versions remain reusable. Writing resumes automatically; **New song** starts fresh.

In Simple, **Help me describe it** suggests fuller wording locally without calling an AI service; edit the result before creating. In Custom → Lyrics, tap **Intro, Verse, Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Bridge** or **Outro** to insert a section label at the cursor. Your lyrics are kept automatically with no recovery question. Optional melody recording is private and only becomes an attached reference after a song is saved. It is not used as music-generation input.

“Make 2 versions” starts off. It creates two separate music requests, clearly warns about double usage/cost, and keeps confirmed versions together so you can listen and favorite the one you prefer. Each request still passes the existing credit check; the first stays saved if the second fails.

The header keeps a **Simple / Advanced** switch available on every screen; it only changes navigation and visibility, not saved work. Screen-specific help sits beside the page title. The first visit shows a short tour; reopen it later through **Settings & Help → Studio guide → Take the quick tour**. The tour explains the workflow but does not generate a song or use provider credits.

Open **My prompts** in Create to search, reuse, or favorite saved lyrics and music directions. **Save this prompt** works whenever you choose; automatic saving is optional and starts **off**. When enabled, it saves the prompt only after a song is confirmed—not after a failed or abandoned request. A favorite change can be undone from its confirmation notice. These controls preserve existing saved prompt records and keep them private.

Give clear language directions if Auto asks for clarification—for example: “A hopeful 45-second pop song about a fresh start. Sing in English with a warm female lead.” A regional style name alone does not always establish the language to sing. Custom → Vocals lets you choose it explicitly without changing your lyrics.

Singing generation uses **ElevenLabs Music**, not Suno and not an internally trained music model. Your own account and provider permissions are required. The disconnected/sketch path is not equivalent to a finished AI-sung song. Creation must keep its existing confirmation and saving protections; changing screens is not permission to submit a paid request.

### My Songs

Find saved songs and recordings, use folders and filters, and reopen existing projects. A song's next steps are:

1. **Listen** — play the saved recording.
2. **Fix a part** — reopen its writing, audio or production tools.
3. **Make video** — carry its audio, lyrics, timing and visuals into Video.
4. **Release** — review its checklist, exports and explicit sharing/publishing controls.

Covers belong to their song. Project workflow, release planning, release tools and the artist-dashboard entry point belong here rather than becoming competing workspaces. Nothing becomes public merely because it is saved or exported. Recovery controls and the disabled permanent-deletion scheduler remain unchanged.

The song actions include **Make it longer** and **Redo a part** through the non-destructive section editor, plus **Make another like this** to prefill Create. Each new passage is separate; the original stays saved. The screen shows a cost warning before another generation.
