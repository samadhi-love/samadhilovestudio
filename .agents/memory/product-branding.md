---
name: Product versus artist branding
description: Keep the app's identity separate from the artist and existing saved work.
---
The approved product rename does not rename the SAMADHI LOVE artist, owner,
artist credits, signature looks or existing saved work. Do not mass-replace
artist names, stored text, technical identifiers or routes during branding.
Keep runtime product labels derived from the shared brand configuration.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested a new product identity while keeping
SAMADHI LOVE as the artist/owner and preserving data, routes and functions.

**How to apply:** Distinguish product navigation, sign-in labels, metadata and
app-generated notices from artist names and user-owned content. Branding changes
in code do not update provider-managed email sender settings automatically.

The DivineSpark welcome is an explicit exception to product-name replacement.
Keep its three lines word-for-word:
- “Welcome to SAMADHI LOVE”
- “A UNIQUE CREATIVE PLATFORM WHERE MUSIC, POSITIVE VIBES AND FREQUENCY MEET”
- “♫ ✧ ❨ Music is for the soul ❩ ✧ ♫”

**Why:** The owner explicitly said “make sure this stay” when approving the
compact banner, despite the app's Versual Studio name.

**How to apply:** Preserve this welcome text during future branding changes.
Its approved homes are after the public landing slogan and at the top of
Create in both Simple and Advanced views, not every Studio screen.

Limit the full digital-hologram treatment to “Your song. Your world. Your video.”,
“Simple view · start with an idea,” and the exact two-sentence description
beginning “Turn your music into a lyric video, animated cover or social short.”
The owner explicitly added those two texts. The longer description must stay
sentence case, comfortably spaced, and decode word by word within about
1.5 seconds. The owner also explicitly added “Originals in motion, one frequency
at a time.” and “Start a song to give your next idea a home.” with the uppercase
slogan treatment.

Use at most one full-hologram attention line per screen. If approved lines
compete on a screen, keep the most prominent full and demote the others to
Level 2. Other short guiding subtitles, section intros and empty-state
invitations may use Level 2: normal font/case/size, soft aqua and very faint
glow only, without decoding, scanlines or animation. Do not tint form labels,
input text/placeholders, buttons, menu items, song titles, lyrics, errors,
warnings, plans/prices, legal text or long unselected paragraphs.

**Why:** The owner approved a moderate two-level system: “ok good idea let's stay moderate,” preserving the earlier “not everywhere just where it needs attention” boundary.

**How to apply:** Choose the main attention line explicitly for each screen and apply the one shared soft-guidance class only to reviewed helper text, never broad paragraph or control selectors. Preserve wording, layout and page palettes.
