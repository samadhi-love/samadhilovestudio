---
name: Product versus artist branding
description: Keep the app's identity separate from the artist and existing saved work.
---
The approved product rename does not rename the SAMADHI LOVE artist, owner,
artist credits, signature looks or existing saved work. Do not mass-replace
artist names, stored text, technical identifiers or routes during branding.
Keep runtime product labels derived from the shared brand configuration.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested a new product identity while keeping
SAMADHI LOVE as the artist/owner and preserving data, routes and functions.

**How to apply:** Distinguish product navigation, sign-in labels, metadata and
app-generated notices from artist names and user-owned content. Branding changes
in code do not update provider-managed email sender settings automatically.

The DivineSpark welcome is an explicit exception to product-name replacement.
Keep its three lines word-for-word:
- “Welcome to SAMADHI LOVE”
- “A UNIQUE CREATIVE PLATFORM WHERE MUSIC, POSITIVE VIBES AND FREQUENCY MEET”
- “♫ ✧ ❨ Music is for the soul ❩ ✧ ♫”

**Why:** The owner explicitly said “make sure this stay” when approving the
compact banner, despite the app's Versual Studio name.

**How to apply:** Preserve this welcome text during future branding changes.
Its approved homes are after the public landing slogan and at the top of
Create in both Simple and Advanced views, not every Studio screen.
