---
name: Language and dialect evidence
description: Product rule for claims about multilingual singing and Lebanese authenticity
---
Do not equate multilingual music input acceptance, speech-language coverage, or passing text fixtures with verified sung pronunciation.

**Why:** The user requires honest broad coverage, not an unverified promise of every language. Lebanese authenticity specifically requires native-speaker listening review.

**How to apply:** Keep music, lyric writing, dialect, alignment and rendering claims separate. Do not mark a singing language tested or native-verified without retained listening evidence. Paid audio tests require approval; no automatic costly retries.

Prefer explicit singing instructions and selected settings over guessing from a regional instrument, genre, the brief's language, or a shared writing system.

**Why:** Creators mix regional sounds with other singing languages. Arabic script also serves Persian and Urdu; a larger language list makes script-only identification less reliable, not more.

**How to apply:** Ask when uncertain, surface conflicting instructions before spending credits, and distinguish singing-performance directions from fixed singer identities.
