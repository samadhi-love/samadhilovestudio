# SAMADHI LOVE: writing guide

**Every page is written for seekers who are ready and questioning: something to learn, look into and discover for themselves. Never a direct attack on anyone or anything, never offensive.**

This is a spiritual site, not a religious one. Mystery-school symbols, the masters, angels and star beings are welcome.

## Voice
- Invite, never command. Uplift, never frighten. Include, never divide.
- Present the teachings as the inner, mystical heart of every tradition, never as an attack on any religion: "Every religion holds a mystical heart. These teachings walk into that heart."
- Describe each tradition in its own words, with its own honorifics. Never compare traditions as better or worse. No politics.
- Label Samadhi's own beliefs as "Samadhi's view". Give exact numbers with their source ("As the mystery schools teach…", "In the Rosicrucian teaching…").
- Karma is the soul's school, not a punishment. No one who suffers deserves it.
- No soul is ever lost; this life is precious. Never words of punishment or hell for those who left early.
- Inner knowing and outer knowledge work together. Keep science where it shows real benefits to people.
- Channelled messages are personal experience; no one must believe them.
- Plain language. Verify every quotation (King James Bible; Pickthall's Qur'an) and list sources.

## Sacred respect
- No pictures of God; no images of the Prophet Muhammad or any prophet in the Islamic context.
- The four-letter Holy Name is never spelled out or given a pronunciation: write "the Holy Name", "HaShem" or "YHVH".
- In The Names of God, holy names sit at the top or centre of their card, never at the foot of a page, in menus, buttons or patterns.
- No nudity; nothing dark or frightening; no skin colour tied to "light" or "dark"; no masks, totems or costumes as decoration; no single disputed holy site as the face of the teachings.

## Every teaching page ends with
- Samadhi's promise, "Take what resonates with your heart; leave the rest.", and the humble note (added automatically).
- On pages about death or the afterlife: the 988 line of care. On health pages: "Spiritual guidance, not a replacement for your doctor." (added automatically).
